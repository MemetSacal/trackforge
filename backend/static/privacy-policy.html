Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

TrackForge ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use the TrackForge mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when using the app:

  • Account information: name, email address, gender, date of birth
  • Health & fitness data: weight, height, body measurements, body fat percentage
  • Activity data: workout sessions, exercise logs, step counts
  • Nutrition data: meal logs, calorie intake, water consumption
  • Sleep data: sleep duration and quality logs
  • Menstrual cycle data (optional, for users who opt in)
  • Photos you voluntarily upload for AI calorie estimation

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the TrackForge service:

  • Providing personalized AI coaching and recommendations
  • Tracking your health and fitness progress over time
  • Generating weekly analytics reports and insights
  • Powering the gamification system (XP, badges, levels)
  • Enabling social features such as leaderboards (only with your consent)
  • Improving app functionality and user experience

3. AI Features & Third-Party Services

TrackForge uses the Anthropic Claude API to power AI coaching features. When you use AI features (weekly analysis, diet advice, workout plans, calorie estimation from photos), relevant data is sent to Anthropic's API for processing.

Anthropic's privacy policy applies to data processed through their API. We do not share your data with any other third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

We also use:

  • Open Food Facts API — for barcode-based nutritional data (no personal data is shared)
  • Render — for backend hosting and database storage

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on our backend servers hosted on Render. We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • JWT-based authentication with secure token storage
  • Password hashing using bcrypt
  • Encrypted storage for sensitive credentials on-device

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below. Upon request, we will delete your data within 30 days.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent for optional features at any time

7. Children's Privacy

TrackForge is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

8. Health Data

TrackForge collects sensitive health and fitness information. This data is used solely to provide the app's core features and is never sold or shared with advertisers. Health data is stored encrypted and is only accessible by the account holder.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
